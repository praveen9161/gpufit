What the model assumes

  • A synchronous job runs at its slowest GPU's pace. Measured in session B: one A10G at 150 W slowed a 2-GPU job to 0.84.
  • With GPUFit, the degraded GPU swaps places with a healthy GPU from the inference side, so training runs at full speed and inference loses only the degraded GPU's shortfall.
  • There is enough inference work to absorb the degraded GPUs, and the swap is quick compared with how long a GPU stays degraded. Job restarts and checkpoint costs are left out.
  • Only stragglers are counted. GPUs that are broken outright, rather than slow, are a different problem.
Where the slowdowns come from

Same GPU, same power cap, very different damage

Session A2 lowered an NVIDIA A10G's power limit from 300 W to 100 W and measured three workloads, three runs each (spread under 0.5%). Heavy matrix work, typical of training, collapses; memory-bound work, typical of LLM inference, does not move.
